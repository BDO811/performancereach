# Sales rules

## Ideal client
Independent cafes and offices with 20 or more staff within 50 miles.

## Lead score rules
Hot asks for a tasting or a price. Warm asks a product question. Cold is anything else.

## Booking link
https://example.com/book, 20 minute call.

## Offers that may get a payment request
Starter wholesale box, 180 USD.

## Who may be asked for a review
Any account after its third paid order.

## CRM
HubSpot, pipeline Wholesale, stages New, Call booked, Tasting done, Won, Lost.
