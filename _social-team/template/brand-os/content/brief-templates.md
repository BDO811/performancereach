# Brief templates

> Read by: content-engine. The orchestrator fills one of these for every job.
> One template per channel. The content engine only writes for channels that have a template here:
> to add a channel, copy a block and change it.
> Lengths are yours to set. Until a length is filled in, the content engine asks before drafting.

## Every brief has these fields
- Audience: who this piece is for (from brand-brain/voice-guide.md)
- One promise: what the reader gets, in one sentence
- Three proof points: IDs from brand-brain/messaging-bank.md
- Keyword: article only, from signals-seo/keyword-map.md
- Call to action: from the offers table in messaging-bank.md
- Visual: what creative-studio needs to make, or "none"
