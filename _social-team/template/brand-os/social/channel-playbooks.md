# Channel playbooks

> Read by: publishing-social, which schedules through Metricool and publishes to WordPress, Google Business Profile,
> Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok.
> One block per channel. Delete the channels you do not use. Nothing goes to a channel that has no block here.

## How to fill a block
- Role: why this channel exists for you, in one line
- Audience: who you reach there
- Formats: what you post there
- Good looks like: the one number you judge it by (define it in reporting/kpi-model.md)
- Do and do not: house rules for this channel only
- Comments: who answers them
