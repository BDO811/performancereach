# Review flow

> Read by: reviews-reputation. Tools: Google Business Profile for reviews, Podium and NiceJob for reviews and review requests, Brand24 for mentions.
> What happens from the moment a review lands to the moment a reply goes up, and how you ask happy customers for reviews.

## From review to reply
1. A review lands (Google Business Profile alert, or a new row in the reviews export).
2. Reviews-reputation reads it, sorts it by the table below and drafts a reply from gbp-response.md.
3. The draft goes where the table says. Nothing is posted until that step is done.
4. Every review and reply is logged at the bottom of this file and in logs/activity.md.
