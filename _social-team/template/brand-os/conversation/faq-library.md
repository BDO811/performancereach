# FAQ library

> Read by: leads-conversations, which answers through ManyChat, Twilio SMS and Front.
> If a question is not in this file, the bot does not answer it. It hands over to a person (see routing-rules.md).
> Every answer here is one you have approved, word for word. Prices, dates and policies are copied from where they really live.

## How to fill it
- Write the question the way customers actually ask it. Add the other ways they ask it in "Also asked as".
- Keep each answer short enough for a chat bubble.
- Put the "Last checked" date on every answer and re-check anything with a price, a date or a policy in it.
