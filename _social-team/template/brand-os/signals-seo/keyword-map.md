> Read by: search-visibility, market-radar. The orchestrator copies the keyword into content briefs from here.

> Fill it from your Google Search Console queries export and your Semrush keyword list.

## Intent labels
| Label | Means the searcher wants to |
|---|---|
| learn | understand something (how to, what is, why) |
| compare | weigh options before buying (best, vs, reviews) |
| buy | act now (price, order, near me, book) |
| find us | reach your brand by name |
