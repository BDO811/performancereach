Unlock the potential of a healthier, safer, and more productive workforce
For industrial enterprises, occupational health and workplace safety are not just operational imperatives—they’re key drivers of performance, cost efficiency, and long-term success. In high-risk, labor-intensive sectors like construction, energy, logistics, and manufacturing, keeping production lines running, meeting shipping goals, or finishing a project on time depends on a workforce that is both mentally sharp and physically resilient.
Sonde offers an innovative, voice-based health solution that enables organizations to stay ahead of workforce health risks—with zero disruption to operations. Our technology focuses on mental and cognitive wellness, allowing companies in physically demanding industries to identify issues like fatigue, burnout, and cognitive strain before they lead to costly accidents, absenteeism, or turnover.

Advance proactive workforce health management
Sonde Health provides a non-invasive, privacy-focused mobile solution that utilizes vocal biomarkers to monitor and improve worker wellness. Our technology analyzes subtle changes in the voice to detect early signs of mental health and cognitive fatigue issues—before they impact your workforce. This early insight is critical in preventing accidents, reducing retraining costs, and improving day-to-day output—especially in fields where consistency and safety are paramount.























  
  






























  
  
    
  


    

    

    
      
    

    


  


  





  

  

  










  
Scale with Sonde’s Advanced Capabilities 
Our vocal biomarker solutions use advanced algorithms to evaluate various aspects of speech. By analyzing these subtle changes in vocal characteristics, our algorithms generate a score that reflects the user’s current relative risk for having mental, cognitive or respiratory health issues.

Maximize Organizational Outcomes with Enterprise Level Insights
