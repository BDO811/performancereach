Engagement through insight

Our voice-powered mental fitness tracking and journaling product can help your users monitor their mental fitness. Objective changes in mental fitness can be measured through changes in vocal features. Without this insight, most people will not act to improve their mental health (97% of large employers offer employee assistance programs (EAPs) and 5.5% of employees use EAPs – Sources: National Business Group on Health; The Employee Assistance Trade Association).
