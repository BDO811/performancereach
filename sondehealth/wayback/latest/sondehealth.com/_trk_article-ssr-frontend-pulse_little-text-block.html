Voice: A Powerful Predictor of Health

Our voice contains crucial health insights. Like changes in body temperature or blood pressure, variations in your voice can signal early health issues. Armed with these insights, proactive health management becomes a reality — sparking timely actions to improve outcomes.

Sonde’s API/SDK platform makes it easy to integrate vocal biomarker analysis into your organization’s health initiatives to gain new insights into users’ mental, cognitive and/or respiratory fitness.

Our vocal biomarkers can help your organization with:

  • early detection and risk stratification

  • increased engagement and enhanced performance

  • reduced burnout and turnover

  • maintaining a healthy workforce

Sonde’s vocal biomarker platform has been meticulously developed by leading scientists and audio signal processing experts to detect subtle changes in voice.
