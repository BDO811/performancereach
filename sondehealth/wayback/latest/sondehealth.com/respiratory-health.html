Our respiratory health product detects and monitors for symptoms of COVID-19, asthma, COPD, and several other pulmonary conditions.

 
 
 

Measures likelihood of having respiratory symptoms like coughing, shortness of breath, difficulty breathing and chest tightness

Respiratory fitness tracking from 6 seconds of voice on a smartphone

Costs a few cents per health check when used daily

Clinical patient engagement rate of 2-3 times per week

2 use cases for Sonde Respiratory Fitness

Remote health monitoring

Health and wellness organizations license Sonde’s respiratory API to help people with chronic respiratory illness to maintain their health and reduce the likelihood of exacerbations

Patient stratification

Companies use Sonde’s respiratory API to find the right patient at the right time to improve marketing/outreach effectiveness, medical workflows or interventions
