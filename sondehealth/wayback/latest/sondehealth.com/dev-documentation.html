 Introduction

 

The Sonde Platform Service API allows our Partners to access Sonde Vocal Biomarkers based Health Check with their own apps. The Sonde Health Check measures the level of wellness and health in a given voice sample. The API is simple to use: Partners can integrate Health Check functionality into their apps in a few hours of development.

Sonde’s platform is HIPAA compliant and has banked over 1,000,000 voice samples from over 80,000 subjects to develop our Health Check models.

 

Getting Started

 

To get started, please complete a company contact information form. We’ll provide you with a “Test” and “Live” key, providing you access to our API, develop and test your apps.

Mental Fitness Score

Your app users give a voice sample and Sonde’s APIs return a mental fitness health check score. Optionally, the API can also return as part of user voice journaling, transcription of their voice sample.

 

Respiratory Symptom Score

Subjects (a subject is an individual performing the activities on the app, such as submitting audio samples) provide default voice samples, and receive an Respiratory Symptoms Risk score back.

 
 

User Questionnaire

As part of the app workflow, subjects are asked demographic or health-based questions (such as age, do you have a cough, etc.) with multiple-choice responses. Based on the subject scores, additional questions may be asked and a score provided to the subject or sent to a Partner’s (Partners use the Sonde Platform to serve their customers) backend system. The questions are customizable.
